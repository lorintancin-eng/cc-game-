## Change Summary

### What Changed

**Decision**: D-B1 Path (a) — Accept Player HP=100; multiply all enemy base `damage` values ×2.0 to compress survival windows.

**Rationale**: Combat GDD OQ-5 identified that HP=100 (shipping code reality) produces survival windows 3× longer than the HP=30 design intent. Path (a) restores the intended pressure curve feel without modifying Player.tscn / CharacterBase.

**Multiplier**: ×2.0 — chosen as a calibration starting point based on:
- Target: Paper Doll should kill a stationary player in ~8-10s (not 17s) — learn-controls phase
- Target: Stone Golem should feel like a genuine threat in 4 hits (~4s) — meaningful danger
- Target: Boss Famine Beast should require active dodging (2.8 hits, 2.4s to die from Boss contact)

## New Per-Phase TTK Budget (post-change)

| Phase | Dominant enemy | dmg (new) | hits-to-die | time to die |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Familiarisation 0:00-1:00 | Paper Doll | **10** | **10** | **8.5s** |
| First Pressure 1:00-2:00 | Wandering Soul | **16** | **6.25** | **5.0s** |
| Risk/Reward 2:00-3:00 | Stone Golem | **24** | **4.17** | **4.2s** |
| Elite Pressure 3:00-4:30 | Shanxiao Elite | **30** | **3.33** | **3.0s** |
| Boss Fight 5:00+ | Famine Beast | **36** | **2.78** | **2.4s** |

Aggregate DPS ceiling (4 attackers) at key phases:
- 4 Paper Dolls: 4 × 10/0.85 ≈ **47 dps** → HP=100 / 47 = **2.1s**
- 4 Wandering Souls: 4 × 16/0.8 = **80 dps** → **1.25s**
- MAX_CONTACT_ATTACKERS=4 ceiling is now MORE critical than before — without it, 4+ enemies = near-instant death.
